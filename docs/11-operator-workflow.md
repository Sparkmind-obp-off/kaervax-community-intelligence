# Operator Workflow

## 1. Radar
See new communities, active communities, new high-intent discussions, opportunities needing review and follow-ups due.

## 2. Inspect
Open the original source and verify the AI interpretation.

## 3. Qualify
Answer:
- What problem exists?
- Who needs it?
- What deliverable could solve it?
- Is there evidence of intent?
- Is engagement appropriate?
- What is the next useful action?

## 4. Act
Choose observe, save, draft reply, ask a useful question, offer a service, refer, connect with a partner or dismiss.

External posting requires user approval unless a separately authorized workflow explicitly permits it.

## 5. Learn
Record no response, conversation, qualified lead, proposal, project or recurring opportunity.

The system improves by learning which signals actually become useful conversations and revenue.
