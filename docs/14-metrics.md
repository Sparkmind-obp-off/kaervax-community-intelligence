# Product Metrics

North-star chain: Communities → Conversations → Signals → Demands → Qualified Opportunities → Actions → Leads → Projects → Revenue.

Operational metrics: time to first useful signal, opportunities reviewed/day, false-positive rate, source freshness and connector reliability.

Commercial metrics: qualified opportunities/week, conversations started, qualified leads, proposals, projects won, recurring customers and attributed revenue.

Do not optimize for raw post volume or community count alone.
